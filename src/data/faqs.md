# Ringlume FAQs

## Can Ringlume answer calls when we're busy or unavailable?

Yes. Ringlume answers 24/7, including when your team is busy, on-site, or off the clock.

## Can Ringlume handle urgent calls?

It can flag urgent business requests and contact your team using your rules. It is not an emergency service.

## What happens when a caller needs professional expertise?

Ringlume collects the details and books a visit or transfers the call. It does not replace professional judgment.

## Can Ringlume book appointments automatically?

Yes. It checks your connected calendar and books appointments using your availability and scheduling rules.

## What information can Ringlume collect from a new lead?

You choose what to collect, such as:

- Name and contact details
- Service address
- Work needed
- Preferred time and urgency

## Can Ringlume answer questions about our business and services?

Yes. It uses your approved services, hours, service areas, prices, and FAQs.

## What if customers ask about pricing?

It shares approved prices or books an estimate. It should not guess.

## Can Ringlume handle different services or types of inquiries?

Yes. Set different questions, booking steps, and handoff rules for each service you offer.

## What happens if Ringlume doesn't know the answer?

It can take a message or hand the call to your team instead of guessing.

## Can important calls be transferred to a real person?

Yes. When enabled, it transfers calls to your team using the rules you choose.

## Will we know what happened after every call?

Yes. Call summaries, recordings, and caller details are available according to your plan and setup.

## Does Ringlume replace my receptionist or office staff?

It handles routine phone calls, not in-person duties. Use it alongside your team or for overflow calls.

## How long does setup take?

Timing depends on your phone system and tools. See [setup details](/terms#setup-time).

## How much does Ringlume cost?

See our [plans](/pricing) for prices, minutes, and setup fees.

## How do I upgrade my subscription plan?

Use our [Contact page](/contact) to request an upgrade. We’ll confirm the change by email.

## How do I cancel my subscription plan?

Request cancellation through our [Contact page](/contact).

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. We’ll email confirmation.
